Original statement on the official download page:
Базы можно использовать свободно в любых целях. Можно указать данный сайт как источник.

English translation for convenience: The databases may be used freely for any
purpose. This website may be cited as the source.

The official distribution page also describes the dictionary as open and freely
redistributable and describes conversion of its structured text to other formats:
https://bkrs.info/w/бкрс/распространение
